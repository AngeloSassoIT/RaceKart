RaceKart

An arcade kart racer built on one number: your best lap.

Build tracks. Beat the clock.

RaceKart is a free arcade kart game for iPhone. Nothing chases you and nothing runs out: you go round again because the last lap was two tenths off. Four circuits ship with the game, and you draw the rest yourself.

  • Four built-in circuits, plus every one you build
  • A full track editor: eight kinds of block
  • The same kart for everyone — the Garage changes looks only
  • Local leaderboards everywhere, worldwide on the Technical Circuit
  • Asynchronous duels and a community circuit vote
  • Free. No ads. No in-app purchases.

RaceKart is made by Angelo Sasso. Not affiliated with any kart manufacturer or racing series.
