Privacy Policy

RaceKart is free, has no advertising, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs of any kind. This page says exactly what leaves your device, and what never does.

What the app publishes, and only when you choose to

Your Game Center nicknameAttached to a circuit you publish and to a duel you enter, so other players can see whose circuit it is and who they are racing. The app reads your alias and never your real name.
An account identifierAn opaque identifier supplied by Apple (iCloud) that links a circuit to its author and stops the same person voting twice. It cannot be traced back to you by us.
Circuits you publishThe track layout itself, plus a lap time and the paintwork of your kart. There is no free-text field anywhere: circuit names are generated by the app, and there is no chat or messaging.

What stays on your device

Your lap times, recorded ghosts, control layout, HUD theme, volumes, blocked authors and the circuits you build all live on your iPhone and are never transmitted. Your player profile syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud account, which we cannot read.

Tracking

Nothing in RaceKart is used to track you. There is no data broker, no advertising identifier, and nothing is ever combined with data from other apps or websites.

Removing what you published

A published circuit can be withdrawn from inside the app, in MY CIRCUITS. If you want everything you have published removed, or you want to know what is stored against your account, write to us and we will do it.

Children

RaceKart is rated for all ages and collects nothing beyond what is described above. It asks for no email address, no account and no personal details.

RaceKart is made by Angelo Sasso. Not affiliated with any kart manufacturer or racing series.
